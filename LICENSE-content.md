## Additional permission for news, research, and education

> In addition to the rights granted by CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, OpenBoardroom permits anyone, including for-profit organizations, to reproduce, quote, and adapt its published content for **news reporting, commentary, scholarly research, and teaching**, without the NonCommercial and ShareAlike conditions, provided that you:
> 1. credit OpenBoardroom and link to the source page;
> 2. mark any changes, and don't alter the content in a way that changes its meaning; and
> 3. don't use the content in, or to build, a brokerage, investment, proxy-voting, or data-redistribution product or service.
>
> This permission doesn't cover bulk or automated access, which requires a written agreement with OpenBoardroom.
